ADMINISTRATIVE ARRANGEMENTS ORDER
I, QUENTIN BRYCE, Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia,
acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, and under Chapter II of
The Constitution, order that the Administrative Arrangements Order made on
14 October 2010 be amended as set out in the Schedule with effect on and from
19 October 2011.
Signed and sealed with the Great Seal of
Australiaon / g October 2011
5)
Quentin Bryce
Governor-General
