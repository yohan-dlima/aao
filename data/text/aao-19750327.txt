AUSTRALIA
ADMINISTRATIVE ARRANGEMENTS
ORDERED BY HIS EXCELLENCY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL
E SIR JOHN ROBERT KERR, the Governor-General of Australia, acting with the advice of the Executive Council—
{a) hereby revoke so much of the Administrative Arrangements Orders made on 8 February 1975, 28 February
1975 and 4 March 1975 as relates to the Department of Customs and Excise;
(b) hereby approve the following administrative arrangements:
@) the matters dealt with by the Department of State specified in the first column of the Schedule to this
Order include the matters specified in the second column of that Schedule and other matters arising
under the enactments administered. by the Minister of State administering that Department;
Gi) the enactments administered by the Minister of State administering that Department are the enactments
specified in the third column of that Schedule and any other enactments, whether passed before or
after the date of this Order, that relate to a matter dealt with by that Department (not being an
enactment specified in the Administrative Arrangements Orders made on 8 February 1975, 28 February
1975 and 4 March 1975 or in this Order in relation to another Depattment);
Gi) the matters dealt with by the Attorney-General’s Department no longer include the following matters,
namely, Law enforcement and Police forces of the Northern Territory of Australia, the Australian
Capital Territory and Norfolk Island; and
Gv) the enactments administered by the Attorney-General no longer include the Commonwealth Police Act
1957-1973 and the matters deait with by the Attorney-General’s Department no longer include matters
arising under that Act; and
(c} hereby approve the continuation in force of the administrative arrangements set out in the Administrative
Arrangements Orders made on 8 February 1975, 28 February t975 and 4 March 1975, except in relation to
the enactments and matters referred to in paragraph (b).
Given under my Hand and the Great Seal of Australia on 27 March 1975.
JOHN R. KERR
