COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA
ADMINISTRATIVE ARRANGEMENTS
ORDERED BY HIS EXCELLENCY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL
| SIR NINIAN MARTIN STEPHEN, Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of
the Federal Executive Council, hereby approve the following administrative arrangements to take effect on and from 3
February 1987 in substitution for the arrangements in operation immediately before the date of this Order:
1. The matters dealt with by each Department of State include the matters specified in the second column of the
Schedule to this Order apposite to the same of that Department in the first column of that Schedule and other matters
arising under the enactments administered by the Minister of State administering that Department.
2, The enactments administered by the Minister of State administering a Department are the enactments specified in
the third column of the Schedule ta thir Osder opposite to the name of that Department and any enactment {whether
passed before or after the date of this Order) that relates to a matter dealt with by that Department, not being an
enactment specified in that column opposite to the name of another Department.
