AUSTRALIA
ADMINISTRATIVE ARRANGEMENTS
ORDERED BY HIS EXCELLENCY
THE ADMINISTRATOR OF THE
GOVERNMENT OF AUSTRALIA
I, SIR ARTHUR RODEN CUTLER, the Administra-
tor of the Government of Australia, acting with the
advice of the Executive Council—
(a) hereby approve the following administrative
arrangements:
(the enactments administered by the Minister
of State for Labor and Immigration no
longer include the Passports Act 1938-1973
and the matters dealt with by the Depart-
ment of Labor and Immigration no longer
include matters arising under that Act;
and
(ii) the enactments administered by _ the
Minister of State for Foreign Affairs
include the Passports Act 1938-1973 and
the matters dealt with by the Department
of Foreign Affairs include matters arising
under that Act; and
(b) hereby approve the continuation in force of the
administrative arrangements set out in the
Administrative Arrangements Orders made on
8 February 1975 and 28 February 1975 except
in relation to the enactments and matters
referred to in paragraph (a).
GIVEN under my Hand and the Great Seal
(LS.) of Australia on 4 March 1975.
A. R. CUTLER
Administrator
By His Excellency’s Command,
E. G. WHITLAM
Prime Minister
(Ex, Min. No. 8)
