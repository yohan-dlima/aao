AUSTRALIA
ADMINISTRATIVE ARRANGEMENTS.
ORDERED BY HIS EXCELLENCY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL
1, SIR JOHN ROBERT KERR, the Governor-General of Australia acting with the advice of the Executive Council—
(a) hereby approve the following administrative arrangements:
(@ the matters dealt with by the Minister of State for Manufacturing Industry include the following matter,
namely, Adjustment assistance to industry;
(il) the enactments administered by the Minister of State for Overseas Trade no longer include the Austratian
Industry Development Corporation Act 1970-1973 and the matters dealt with by the Department of
Overseas Trade no longer include matters arising under that Act and the following matter, namely,
Adjustment assistance to industry;
(iii) the enactments administered by the Prime Minister no longer include the industries Assistance Com-
mission Act 1973 and the Prices Justification Act 1973-1974 and the matters deait with by the Depart-
‘ment of the Prime Minister and Cabinet no longer include matters arising under those Acts;
(iv) the enactments administered by the Special Minister of State include the Industries Assistance Com-
mission Act 1973 and the Prices Justification Act 1973-1974 and the matters deait with by the Depart-
ment of the Special Minister of State include matters arising under those Acts; and
(¥) the enactments administered by the Treasurer include the Australian Industry Development Corporation
‘Act 1970-1973 and the matters dealt with by the Department of the Treasury include matters arising
under that Act; and
(b) hereby approve the continuation in force of the administrative arrangements set out in the Administrative
‘Arrangements Order made on 1 October 1974 except in relation to the enactments and matters referred to
in paragraph (a).
Given under my Hand and the Great Seal of Australia on 11 December 1974,
JOHN R. KERR
