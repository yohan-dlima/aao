DELEGATION TO PRESIDE OVER MEETINGS OF THE FEDERAL EXECUTIVE COUNCIL.
[I* pursuance of the provisions of section 126 of the Constitution of the Commonwealth of Australia, and of clause 6 of
the Letters Patent constituting the office of Governor-General, dated 29th October, 1900, I hereby appoint
Senator the Honourable Joseph Silver Collings, Vice-President of the Executive Council, to be my deputy to summon
meetings of the Executive Council, and in the event of my inability to be present, to preside over meetings of the Executive
Council and signify approval of the proceedings at such meetings.
If, for any reason, the Vice-President of the Executive Council is unable to attend, the Senior Member of the -
Executive Council who is present is hereby authorized to exercise the authority herein vested in the Vice-President.
This appointment supersedes the appointment dated 6th July, 1945, of the Honourable John Albert Beasley, M.P.,
which appointmenist hereby terminated.
Dated this thirteenth day of July, 1945.
HENRY,
Governor-General.
