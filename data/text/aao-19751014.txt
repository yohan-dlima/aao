AUSTRALIA
ADMINISTRATIVE ARRANGEMENTS
ORDERED BY HIS EXCELLENCY THE
GOVERNOR-GENERAL
I, SIR JOHN ROBERT KERR, the Governor-General
of Australia, acting with the advice of the Executive
Councit—
(a) hereby approve the following Administrative
Arrangements:
(i) the enactments administered by the
Attorney-General no longer include the
Trade Practices Act 1974-1975 Part V, and
the matters dealt with by the Attorney.
General's Department no longer include
matters arising under that Part of that Act;
and
(i) the enactments administered by _ the
Minister for Science and Consumer Affairs
- include the Trade Practices Act 1974-1975,
Part V, and the matters dealt with by the
Department of Science and Consumer
Affairs include matters arising under that
Part of that Act; and
(b) hereby approve the continuation in force of the
Administrative Arrangements set out in the
Administrative Arrangements Orders made on
r( ) 1 July 1975, 26 September 1975 and 7 October
1975 subject to the changes made by this Order.
GIVEN under my Hand and the Great Seal
{L.S.) of Australia on 14 October 1975,
JOHN R. KERR
Governor-General
By His Excellency’s Command,
E. G. WHITLAM
Prime Minister.
