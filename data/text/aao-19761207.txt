COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA
ACTS INTERPRETATION ACT 1901
ORDER UNDER SECTION 1984
WHEREAS in the provisions of the Acts and Regulations specified in the first column of the Schedule and in
provisions of other instruments made under those Acts and Regulations there are references to the Department of
ihe Treasury and to the office of the Secretary to the Department of the Treasury (being an office the holder of which
is, by virtue of section 25 of the Public Service Act 1922, the Permanent Head of the Department of the Treasury}:
AND WHEREAS, by virtue of administrative arrangements ordered by the Governor-General on 7 December 1976,
there is a change in the matters dealt with by the Department of the Treasury but the name of that Department and
the name of that office are not changed:
NOW THEREFORE I, SIR JOHN ROBERT KERR, the Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia,
acting with the advice of the Federal Executive Council, in pursuance of sub-sections (2) and (3) of section 19a of
the Acts Interpretation Act 1901, by this Order direct that each of those provisions shall have effect as if there were
substituted for the reference specified in the second column of the Schedule in relation to the provision the reference
specified in the third column of that Schedule opposite to the first-mentioned reference.
