COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA
ADMINISTRATIVE ARRANGEMENTS,
ORDERED BY HIS EXCELLENCY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL
I, SIR ZELMAN COWEN, the Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice of the
Federal Executive Council—
(a) hereby revoke so much of the Administrative Arrangements Order made on 20 December 1977 as relates to
the Department of National Development;
(b} hereby approve the following administrative arrangements:
(i) the matters dealt with by the Department of State specified in the first column of the Schedule to this
Order include the matiers specified in the second column of that Schedule and other matters arising
under the enactments administered by the Minister of State administering that Department;
(ii) the enactments administered by the Minister of State administering the Department specified in the
first column of the Schedule to this Order are the enactments specified in the third column of the
Schedule to this Order and any other enactment, whether passed before or after the date of this Order,
that relates to a matter dealt with by that Department (not being an enactment specified in relation
to another Department in so much of the Administrative Arrangements Order made on 20
December 1977 as is continued in force by this Order or in this Order);
iil) the enactments administered by the Minister of State for Trade and Resources no longer include the
Coal Research Assistance Act 1977 and the Liguid Fuel (Defence Stocks) Act 1949, and the matters
dealt with by the Department of Trade and Resources no longer include matters arising under those
C ) cts;
(iv) the enactments administered by the Minister of State for Finance include the Tasmania Grant (The
Mount Lyell Mining and Railway Company Limited) Act 1977, and the matters dealt with by the Depart-
ment of Finance include matters arising under that Act;
{v) the matters dealt with by the Department of Science no longer include the Australian Science and
Technology Council;
(vi) the matters dealt with by the Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet include the Australian
Science and Technology Council;
(vif) the enactments administered by the Minister of State for Social Security no longer include the /mmi-
gration (Guardianship of Children) Act 1946, and the matters dealt with by the Department of Social
Security no longer include matters arising under that Act;
(viii) the enactments administered by the Minister of State for Immigration and Ethnic Affairs include the
Immigration (Guardianship of Children) Act 1946, and the matters dealt with by the Department of
Immigration and Ethnic Affairs include matters arising under that Act;
(ix) notwithstanding anything to the contrary in the Administrative Arrangements Order made on 20
December 1977, the enactments administered by the Department of Foreign Affairs include the Papua
New Guinea (Staffing Assistance} Act 1973, except to the extent administered by the Minister for
Finance;
x) notwithstanding anything to the contrary in the Administrative Arrangements Order made on 20
December 1977, the enactments administered by the Department of Primary Industry include the
Continental Shelf (Living Natural Resources) Act 1968, except to the extent administered by the Minister
for Home Affairs, the Minister for the Capital Territory and the Minister for the Northern Territory;
and
(c) hereby approve the continuation in force, subject to paragraphs (a) and (b) of this Order, of the administrative
arrangements set out in the Administrative Arrangements Order made on 20 December 1977.
ao) (LS) Given under my Hand and the Great Seal of Australia on 23 February 1978.
ZELMAN COWEN
Governor-General
By His Exceltency’s Command,
MALCOLM FRASER
Prime Minister
