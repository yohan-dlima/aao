CREATION OF DEPARTMENT AND RELATED MATTERS
HIS Excellency the Governor-General in Council has been pleased:
(@) to approve of the establishment, in pursuance of section 64 of the Constitution, of a Department of State
namely, The Department of Productivity
{b) to approve of the creation, in pursuance of the Public Service Act 1922, of the office of The Secretary to The
Department of Productivity to be the Permanent Head of The Department of Productivity.

I, SIR JOHN ROBERT KERR, the Governor-General of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the advice
of the Federal Executive Council—
{a) hereby revoke so much of the Administrative Arrangements Order made on 5 October 1976 as relates to the
Department of Industry an¢ Commerce;
(b) hereby approve the following administrative arrangements:
(@® the matters dealt with by the Department of Business and Consumer Affairs no longer include Patents

paragraph (i);
Gi) the enactments administered by the Minister of State for Business and Consumer Affairs no fonger

Department; and
