Minister of State for Foreign Affairs, and to administer the Department of State connected with that office.

ADMINISTRATIVE ARRANGEMENTS
ORDER BY HIS EXCELLENCY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL
I, SIR PAUL MEERNAA CAEDWALLA HASLUCK, the Governor-General in and over the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the
advice of the Federal Executive Council, hereby amend the Administrative Arrangements Order made on the sixteenth day of April, One thousand
nine hundred and seventy, by omitting the words “The Department of External Affairs” in the first column of the Schedule to that Order and
inserting in their stead the words “The Department of Foreign Affairs”.
Given under my Hand and the Great Seal of the Commonwealth, this sixth day of November, One thousand
(ws) nine hundred and seventy.
PAUL HASLUCK
* Governor-General
By His Excellency’s Command,
J. G. GORTON
Prime Minister
