COMMONWEALTH OF AUSTRALIA
ADMINISTRATIVE ARRANGEMENTS,
ORDERED BY HIS EXCELLENCY THE
GOVERNOR-GENERAL
], SIR JOHN ROBERT KERR, the Governor-General
of the Commonwealth of Australia, acting with the
advice of the Federal Executive Council—
(a) hereby approve the following administrative
arrangements:
(i) the enactments administered by the
Minister of State for Administrative Ser-
vices include the Historic Shipwrecks Act
1976; and
(ii) the matters dealt with by the Department
of Administrative Services include matters
arising under that Act; and
(b) hereby approve the continuation in force,
subject to this Order, of so much of the
administrative arrangements set out in the
Administrative Arrangements Orders made on
5 October 1976, 8 November 1976 and 7
December 1976 as was in force immediately
before the date of this Order.
GIVEN under my Hand and the Great Seal
(LS) of Australia on 16 December 1976,
