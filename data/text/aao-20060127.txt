His Excellency the Governor-General directs it to be notified, for general information, that:
a) he has accepted the resignations of:
Senator the Honourable Robert Murray Hill as Minister for Defence; Senator the
Honourable Kay Christine Lesley Patterson as Minister for Family and Community
Services; and the Honourable Warren George Entsch MP as Parliamentary Secretary to
the Minister for Industry, Tourism and Resources;
