No. 41.) CANBERRA, FRIDAY, 61H JUNE. — [9952.
- ABOLITION OF DEPARTMENT AND ESTABLISHMENT OF DEPARTMENT.
Ae T IS Excellency the Governor-General in council, in pursuance of Section 64 of the Constitutioonf the Commonwealth
of Australia, has been pleased to abolish the following Department of State of the Commonwealth, viz. :—
The Department, of Works and Housing,

The Department of Works.
